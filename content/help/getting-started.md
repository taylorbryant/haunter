Haunter keeps notes, tasks, and drawings in the same workspace. Start with a page for something you are working on, then add tasks and a canvas when you need them.

## Sign in and choose a workspace

1. [Open Haunter](/sign-in) and enter your email address.
2. Enter the sign-in code sent to your email. If your account is waiting for access, follow the message on the sign-in screen.
3. Use the workspace name at the top of the sidebar to switch workspaces. Choose **New workspace** there to create another space.

A workspace contains its own pages, tasks, canvases, and members. Check the workspace name before creating something or searching. On a phone, open the sidebar using the button at the top of the screen.

## Create your first page

1. In the sidebar, select the **New page** button beside **Pages**.
2. Give the page a title, such as “Plan my week.”
3. Click or tap below the title and start writing.
4. Type `/` on a new line to open the block menu. Try a heading, a task, or a canvas.

Changes save automatically. Watch the save status before closing the page, especially if your connection drops. [Learn about saving and recovery](/guides/saving-and-recovery#recover-work-that-has-not-synced).

Your first workspace includes **Start here** and **This week** as example pages. Edit them or move them to Trash when you no longer need them.

## Find your way around

- **Home** brings together overdue tasks, tasks due today and in the coming week, favorites, and recently viewed pages and canvases.
- **Pages** shows your notes and their nested subpages. Expand a page to see its children.
- **Tasks** collects tasks from pages and tasks created on their own.
- **Canvases** lists standalone drawings. Drawings inserted into a page stay with that page.
- **Search** finds pages, tasks, and canvases in the current workspace.
- **Trash** holds pages you have removed until you restore or permanently delete them.

Use the star on a page or canvas to keep it in your favorites. Favorites are personal to you.

## Make it comfortable

Open your account menu at the bottom of the web app's sidebar, then choose **Settings → Appearance** to change your theme. You can also set light, dark, or system mode from the account menu.

The sidebar button at the top of the workspace gives you more room to write. On small screens, it opens and closes navigation.

## Try a small workflow

Write three priorities on your new page. Add a task for the first priority, assign it to yourself, and give it a due date of today. Open **Home** to see it under Today, then follow its page link back to your notes.

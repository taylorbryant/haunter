A workspace holds your **pages** (notes), **tasks** (things to do), and **canvases** (drawings and diagrams).

## Sign in and choose a workspace

1. [Open Haunter](/sign-in) and enter your email address.
2. Enter the six-digit code sent to your email and follow any account setup prompts. If your account is waiting for access, follow the message on screen.
3. Select the workspace name at the top of the sidebar to switch workspaces or choose **New workspace**.

Each workspace has its own content and members. Check its name before creating or searching. On a phone, use the button at the top of the screen to open the sidebar.

## Create your first page

1. In the sidebar, select the **New page** button beside **Pages**.
2. Give the page a title, such as “Plan my week.”
3. Click or tap below the title and start writing.
4. Type `/` on a new line to open the insert menu. Choose **Heading 1** to add a section, or **Task** to add something you need to do.

Changes save automatically. Check the save status in the page header before closing, especially if your connection drops. [Learn how to check that your work is saved](/guides/saving-and-recovery#check-that-your-work-is-saved).

Your first workspace includes **Start here** and **This week** as example pages. Edit them or move them to Trash when you no longer need them.

## Find your way around

- **Home** brings together your assigned tasks, favorites, and recently viewed pages and canvases.
- **Pages** shows your notes and their nested subpages. Expand a page to see its children.
- **Tasks** collects tasks from pages and tasks created on their own.
- **Canvases** lists standalone drawings. Drawings inserted into a page stay with that page.
- **Search** finds pages, tasks, and canvases in the current workspace.
- **Trash** holds pages you have removed until you restore or permanently delete them.

Use the star in a page header or beside a canvas in the Canvases list to add it to your favorites. Favorites are personal to you.

## Make it comfortable

Open your account menu at the bottom of the web app's sidebar, then choose **Settings → Appearance** to change your theme. You can also set light, dark, or system mode from the account menu.

The sidebar button at the top of the workspace gives you more room to write. On small screens, it opens and closes navigation.

## Try a small workflow

Write three priorities on your page. Type `/` and choose **Task** for the first priority. Assign it to yourself and set its due date to today. Open **Home** to find it under Today, then follow its page link back to your notes.

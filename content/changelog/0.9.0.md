## Collaboration

- Write on the same page or draw on the same canvas with other workspace members, with changes appearing as you work.
- See collaborators' cursors as you write and draw together.
- On a canvas, press / on a desktop keyboard to send a message beside your cursor.

## Connection recovery

- Page edits made during a connection interruption are kept in this browser and merge when you reconnect.
- Canvas edits sync when your connection returns. Keep the canvas tab open until it reconnects.

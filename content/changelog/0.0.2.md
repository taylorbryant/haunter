## New

- Invite other people to a workspace and choose whether they can view or edit.
- Assign tasks to members of a shared workspace.
- Publish a read-only page link for people outside your workspace.
- Attach files to pages and restore an earlier version from page history.
- Edit page content, titles, and canvases together in real time with collaborator cursors.

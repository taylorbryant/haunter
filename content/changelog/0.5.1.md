## Improved

- Quotes, toggle controls, and empty-toggle prompts now match the active theme.
- Page formatting and block menus are simpler, with inconsistent color controls removed.

## Fixed

- Canvases keep text and shape sizing consistent when entering and leaving fullscreen.
- Canvases recover when drawing fonts take too long to load.
- Formatting tooltips now connect cleanly to the control they describe.

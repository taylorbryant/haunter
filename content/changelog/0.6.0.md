## New

- Complete assigned, reminded, and overdue tasks without leaving the notification center.
- Snooze reminders and overdue alerts for 15 minutes, one hour, or until tomorrow morning.

## Improved

- Snoozed alerts return as unread and send a new push notification when push is enabled.
- Completed alerts stay in notification history so it is clear what was handled.

## In your AI app

- Open pages, tasks, and canvases alongside your conversation in AI apps that support MCP Apps.
- Use Home, favorites, page history, and Trash in the panel. Your theme is remembered.
- Select text or shapes to give your assistant context.
- Agent changes to tasks and favorites appear without a refresh.

## More capable agents

- Ask agents to edit blocks and tables, move content, and attach files.
- Arrange canvases with groups, frames, alignment, and spacing. Insert library components and images.
- Manage standalone canvases and favorites, follow backlinks, browse Trash, and restore page or canvas versions.
- See agent activity on pages and canvases, with brief highlights on created or updated shapes.

## Search and sharing

- Search page text, task titles, and canvas titles or text. Filter by type, or jump to a matching shape, including drawings inside pages.
- Public page links include a preview image with the title and icon in apps that support link previews.

## On mobile

- Format text from a toolbar above the keyboard. Active formatting is clearer across themes.
- Shared pages and canvases fit small screens better. Trash gives titles and actions more room.
- Delete a code block from its options menu.

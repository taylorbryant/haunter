## New

- Add a reminder to any dated task: at its due time, 15 minutes before, 1 hour before, or 1 day before.
- Receive reminders in Haunter and, when enabled, through push notifications.

## Improved

- Date-only tasks can remind you at 9:00 AM in your notification timezone.
- Reminder settings follow the task when it is rescheduled or reassigned.

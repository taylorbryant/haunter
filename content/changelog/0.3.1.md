## New

- Get an in-app notification when another workspace member assigns you an open task.
- Open an assignment notification to jump directly to its page or to My Tasks.

## Improved

- Assignments made from pages, task lists, imports, restored versions, and connected AI clients now appear in the same notification inbox.
- Choose whether to receive new task assignment notifications from Notifications settings.
